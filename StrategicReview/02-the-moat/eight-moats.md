# Eight Moats — which one is ours?

*Session: 12 September 2026.*

You don't need all eight. You need one that is actually real.

| Moat | Today | In reach | Verdict |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Data** | 1 | 2 | Proprietary but inert. The rating engine is hand-tuned constants — it does not learn from a single row collected, so volume never improves the product. A record that compounds *for one athlete* is a switching cost, not a data moat. |
| **Workflow** | 2 | **4** | Shallow at the club (six sliders, minutes a week), and the deep processes — registration, payments, scheduling — were deliberately ceded. But a *school's* reporting cycle is the stickiest process in the sector. Embedded in pastoral reporting, this gets real. |
| **Regulatory** | **0** | **5** | Nothing built — no entity, no terms, no consent capture, no DPIA. Yet this is the single most defensible position available. See below. |
| **Distribution** | 1 | 3 | The business-model matrix says "Nothing." Latent: one institution = 10 coaches + 200 families, and in small markets four reference schools *is* a channel. |
| **Ecosystem** | 0 | 1 | No API, no integrations, no partners. Not a path for a product this size. |
| **Network** | 1 | **4** | Stronger than it looks, and clustered — see below. |
| **Physical** | 0 | 0 | None, correctly. This is exactly **Veo's** moat — a camera already on the touchline. Not a fight to pick. |
| **Scale** | 1 | 1 | Works *against* Trak. Per-assessment model cost (the Touchline prototype) means unit economics degrade with volume. |

**Headline: there is no moat today.** Nothing scores above 2. What exists is one genuinely
defensible position, and it is not the one you'd expect.

---

## The super moat: Regulatory × Network

Neither half is a moat alone. Regulatory alone is a compliant app nobody uses. Network alone is a
popular app no school can lawfully adopt and no institution accepts. Multiplied, they produce
something no competitor can copy at any price:

> **The only record of a young athlete's performance and conduct that a school can lawfully hold,
> and that another institution will accept.**

**Why the regulatory half is unusually strong.** Trak processes children's behavioural data in
schools — GDPR Article 8 consent, likely special-category data, DPIA, safeguarding, retention,
residency, versioned parental consent, a school pilot agreement. Education procurement is a
*gate*, not a preference. That stack is slow, expensive, unglamorous and permanent once built. And
**Veo and Hudl will never build it** — a camera company has no commercial reason to take on
minors' behavioural-data liability. This is not defending a feature; it is standing somewhere they
will not follow.

**Why the network half is real, not wishful.** Two effects, both clustered rather than global:

- *Continuity* — a transferring player lands at a new club whose coach is already on Trak, so the
  record survives. In small, dense markets players circulate inside a limited set of academies —
  the textbook condition for reaching critical density cheaply. One city could tip it.
- *Acceptance* — a passport is only evidence if someone accepts it. Every institution that accepts
  it makes it more valuable to every athlete and every other institution. That is a credential
  network, the same shape as a music grade, a coaching licence, or the Duke of Edinburgh Award.

Which reframes the business: **the moat is not software, it is who accepts the certificate.** Trak
is closer to a credential body than a SaaS product.

---

## What this changes

1. **The lawyer meeting is not overhead — it is moat construction.** The compliance work sitting
   in the blocked column *is* the asset. Resource it as the product, not a prerequisite.
2. **Density beats reach.** One city, saturated, beats four countries sampled. A "global passport"
   with no local density is a diary with ambitions.
3. **Get one institution to formally *accept* the passport** — a written line in an academy's or
   school's own process saying they read it at intake. One signature like that is worth more than
   a thousand users.

**Caveat:** both halves were at zero. This is a moat that can be built, not one that exists, and
the window is open only while Veo is busy selling cameras.
