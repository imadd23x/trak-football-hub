# Games 7/8 (TRAK-289) plan: publish the evaluation and close the loop

**Issue:** TRAK-289 (spec TRAK-282). **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-games-tab-design.md`.
**Branch:** `TRAK-289-publish-evaluation` from main. One PR. The scheduled share and the morning nudge are TRAK-290.

**Decisions (Imad, question tool):**

- 27 Oct: publishing writes the result through the existing match log, so every screen that reads matches keeps
  working.
- 28 Oct: no match rating for a Games match. Every rating reader counts rated matches only.
- 29 Oct: a match made before 29 Oct has no competition, so the Publish step asks League, Tournament or Friendly;
  Publish stays off until it's picked, and publishing saves it on the match. Home or away, which the match log also
  needs and an older match may lack, is asked the same way.

## Tasks

1. **Migration `20261029170000_game_publish.sql`:**
   - `matches.computed_rating` drops NOT NULL, so a Games match has no rating;
   - `game_review_for_family` (the player and their guardians) and `game_review_for_player` (the note, the player
     only), SELECT only, read through `trak_private.family_reads_game_copy`;
   - `publish_game_evaluation(event, competition, home_away)`, the only write path, in one transaction: the coach,
     the switch, an uncancelled match past full time, a saved draft not yet published, a score, every "Did the plan
     work?" answer, a competition and home or away. It writes the match session and attendance, the review copies
     for each child who played and can still be recorded, and the match log through `log_match_for_player` with no
     rating; it returns who it skipped;
   - `edit_logged_match` refuses a match whose evaluation is published.
   - **Suite:** `supabase/tests/games_publish.sql`, 28 checks: before and after publishing, as player A, player B,
     A's guardian, another coach and a child waiting for consent; strength, scouting and how they scored copied
     nowhere; published is final.

   *Verify:* `node scripts/test-db.mjs --games-publish`, then `--all`. Seven of eight weakened copies of the function
   fail it; the eighth (no "already published" check) is still refused by the "already logged" check. Without the
   `edit_logged_match` refusal, its two checks fail.
2. **The child's side:** `GameReviewSection` (After the game, TRAK-282's words) on `PlayerEvent` and `ParentEvent`;
   the note in its tinted panel for the player only, the parent's line instead; the event card's "Coach's review";
   match history shows a Games match as "Coach's review" with no band and opens its event page; rating readers skip
   matches with no rating.

   *Verify:* `player-game-review-trak289.test.tsx`, `parent-game-review-trak289.test.tsx`, `records.test.ts`.
3. **The loop closes:** the state Published; Home's week and Needs you close; Schedule's View review replaces Edit
   match on a published Games match.

   *Verify:* `loop.test.ts`, `coach-week.test.ts`.
4. **The Publish step and the published view:** the summary ("14 players · 3 changed from “Met” · 5 notes"), who sees
   what, the preview (As the player or As the parent, any player who played, drawn by `GameReviewSection`), Publish to
   N players and families (off until every answer, the competition and home or away are in), Save draft · sleep on
   it; once published, the success alert, the History (Arc's free `timeline`) and the preview, and nothing to edit.

   *Verify:* `evaluation.test.ts`, `game-publish-trak289.test.tsx`.
5. **Browser:** `e2e/games.spec.ts` publishes at 390 and 1440 px and opens the player's After the game. The strict
   backends of `arc-refresh-screens`, `parent-family`, `parent-assessment-history` and `team-kits` serve the two
   review tables.
6. **Docs:** the design doc's review tables, publish function, decisions and rollback note, and the full Games run in
   `docs/rehearsal-script.html`.
