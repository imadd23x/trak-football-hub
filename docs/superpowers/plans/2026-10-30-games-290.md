# Games 8/8 (TRAK-290) plan: Share at… and the morning-after nudge

**Issue:** TRAK-290 (spec TRAK-282). **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-games-tab-design.md`.
**Branch:** `TRAK-290-scheduled-share` from main. One PR. No background job.

**Decisions:**

- TRAK-290: "Likely no background job is needed": a plan's copies carry the time they go out, and the read rules open
  them from then. Confirmed with the existing rules: no job.
- The design doc: a copy is out from `least(share time, kick-off)`, so a match moved earlier than a scheduled share
  still shows the plan at kick-off; the nudge is the first 09:00 after the draft's last save, on the academy's clock.
- Imad, 29 Oct (question tool): a scheduled plan goes out as saved. Saving before the time rewrites what waits; nobody
  has read it, so nobody reads "Updated" and a waiting plan has no "Changes not shared".
- Arc: `split-button` for Share now · Share at… (its docs: a share action with one usual pick and variants behind
  the chevron). Already in Trak (CoachAssessmentCard), React 18 safe; `date-picker` and `time-picker` too. No Pro
  additions; the Pro readiness block gains an alternatives slot.
